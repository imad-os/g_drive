MyDrive Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · App: MyDrive for My PC · Terms of Service

MyDrive is a file explorer for Google Drive that runs inside My PC on TVs, phones and computers. This policy explains what MyDrive accesses, where it is kept, and how you stay in control. MyDrive has no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What MyDrive accesses

  • Your Google account's basic profile (name, e-mail address and account ID), through the openid, email and profile permissions. It is used only to show which account is linked and to tell several linked accounts apart.
  • Google Drive files that MyDrive created, or that you chose for it, through the drive.file permission. MyDrive cannot see the rest of your Drive. You choose existing files on the "Add files" page (Google Picker).
  • For those files: names, types, sizes, dates, thumbnails and contents, to list, preview and play them; and the changes you ask for (create folder, rename, move, copy, star, move to Trash, restore, upload).

Where it is kept

  • Sign-in credential. When you link an account, Google gives MyDrive a refresh token. MyDrive saves it, with your account's name, e-mail and ID, in the My PC save data of your My PC profile. My PC is the platform MyDrive runs in. My PC keeps this save data for each profile; when your profile is a signed-in My PC player account, it is synced so your linked account follows you to your other devices. Other My PC profiles cannot read it. My PC may also include it in that device's backup.
  • Short-lived access tokens, file lists, thumbnails and the parts of files being viewed or played are kept only in memory and are gone when MyDrive closes. Nothing is written to a public place.
  • Preferences (view, sort order, last folder) are saved in the same My PC save data.

Sharing

MyDrive does not sell, rent or share your data. It talks only to Google (accounts and Drive) and, through My PC, to nobody else. My PC may send error reports about app failures; MyDrive never puts names, file names, tokens or file contents in them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MyDrive's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the file-explorer features you see, is not used for advertising, is not transferred to others except as needed for those features or by law, and is not read by humans unless you ask for help with a specific file and agree, or the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Disconnect in MyDrive (Google accounts screen) deletes the saved credential and account details from your My PC profile and asks Google to cancel MyDrive's access.
  • You can also cancel access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting your My PC profile or its data removes everything MyDrive saved there.
  • Your files stay in your Google Drive; MyDrive changes them only when you ask.

Children

MyDrive is not directed at children under 13 and uses Google accounts under Google's own age rules.
