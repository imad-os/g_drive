MyDrive Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026 · App: MyDrive for My PC · Terms of Service

MyDrive is a file explorer for Google Drive that runs inside My PC on TVs, phones and computers. This policy explains what MyDrive accesses, where it is kept, and how you stay in control. MyDrive has no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who signs in to Google

Since version 1.1.0 MyDrive has no Google sign-in of its own. You sign in to Google Drive once in My PC (Control Panel > Google Drive, or the "Sign in to Google Drive" button in MyDrive, which opens My PC's sign-in screen). My PC keeps that sign-in for your My PC profile and sends every Google Drive request for MyDrive. The first time, My PC asks whether MyDrive may use your Google Drive; you can take that back in Control Panel > Google Drive. MyDrive never receives a Google password, token or client secret, and it stores none.

What MyDrive accesses

  • Your Google account's name, e-mail address and picture, as My PC passes them on, only to show which account is in use.
  • The Google Drive files My PC may open: the whole Drive when you signed in to My PC with full access, or only the files My PC and its apps created and the files you chose on the "Add files" page (Google Picker).
  • For those files: names, types, sizes, dates, thumbnails and contents, to list, preview and play them; and the changes you ask for (create folder, rename, move, copy, star, move to Trash, restore, upload).

USB drives (on a TV)

On a TV, MyDrive can show the USB drives plugged into it and ask My PC to copy files between them and your Google Drive. My PC asks once whether MyDrive may use your USB drives. MyDrive sees only the names, sizes and folders of the files on the drives; My PC reads and writes them for a copy, on the TV itself. Nothing on a USB drive goes anywhere but to the Google Drive folder you choose.

Where it is kept

  • No credentials. MyDrive keeps no Google token. Accounts linked by MyDrive before version 1.1.0 are taken over by My PC (same Google project), and MyDrive then deletes its own copy from your My PC save data.
  • File lists, thumbnails and the parts of files being viewed or played are kept only in memory and are gone when MyDrive closes. Nothing is written to a public place.
  • Preferences (view, sort order, last folder) are saved in the My PC save data of your profile.

Sharing

MyDrive does not sell, rent or share your data. It talks to nobody directly: My PC sends its Google Drive requests to Google. My PC may send error reports about app failures; MyDrive never puts names, file names, tokens or file contents in them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MyDrive's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the file-explorer features you see, is not used for advertising, is not transferred to others except as needed for those features or by law, and is not read by humans unless you ask for help with a specific file and agree, or the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Control Panel > Google Drive in My PC: take back MyDrive's permission, switch the Google account or sign out.
  • You can also cancel access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting your My PC profile or its data removes everything MyDrive saved there.
  • Your files stay in your Google Drive; MyDrive changes them only when you ask.

Children

MyDrive is not directed at children under 13 and uses Google accounts under Google's own age rules.
